Document Guide

This appraisal document is designed to increase the fairness in our appraisal process. It is expected that every team lead sets KPIs for their team members at the beginning of every appraisal cycle.

📊
Section 1 — KPI Review

KPIs per objective should be between 2–4. Functional objectives are related to your departmental objectives. Weights denote level of importance — total must equal 100%. Deliverable KPIs use a completion level (Fully / Largely / Partially / Not Delivered) instead of a numerical target.
